Avto Uploader

Avto Uploader is a private, single-user desktop tool. Its owner uses it to publish the owner's own original long-form videos (narrated fiction stories in Russian) to the owner's own YouTube channel.

This app uses YouTube API Services.

By using Avto Uploader you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data accessed through YouTube API Services is handled as described in our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video file from the owner's computer to the owner's channel (videos.insert) with its title, description, tags and the "altered or synthetic content" disclosure.
  • Sets the custom thumbnail for that video (thumbnails.set).
  • Adds the video to the owner's playlists (playlistItems.insert).
  • Reads the owner's channel status and public video statistics to plan the next videos (channels.list, videos.list, search.list, YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel only).

Who can use it

Only the owner. The app is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not used to access anyone else's account or data. It signs in with Google OAuth 2.0 and runs locally on the owner's computer.
